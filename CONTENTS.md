# Research catalog

Every structural result below concerns a hypothetical strongly regular graph with parameters $(99,14,1,2)$. The ledger gives the assumptions and stopping points of the construction and solver runs. [Research ledger](STATE.md)

## Grids and triangles

- [At most one isomorphic partner per grid](research/grids-and-triangles/rook_partner_bound.md) combines a complete 59-class interface census with an independently audited, checked integer completion proof. No red-triangle assumption is used. [Integer model and certificate](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_iso_joint_integer.md)
- [The complete two-arm census](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_interfaces.md) excludes 58 classes by exact binary quota propagation and records incidence controls for the last. The integer edge-completion proof excludes that last class.
- [Star and four-cycle obstructions](research/grids-and-triangles/rook_interface_star.md) and the [five-cycle obstruction](research/grids-and-triangles/rook_interface_five_cycle.md) retain independent weaker arguments using only visible common-neighbor quotas and exact interface domains.
- [Completion constraints](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_iso_completion.md) give the forced 81-column incidence of two isomorphic bases and an exact exclusion of one fixed three-base incidence. The [joint real relaxation](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_iso_joint_incidence.md) has rational feasible witnesses, showing where integrality is needed.
- [Single-pair completion](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_iso_pair_integer.md), [duplicate-label ordering](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_iso_pair_integer_lex.md), and the [Hall-strengthened run](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_iso_pair_integer_hall.md) preserve three bounded UNKNOWN outcomes and exact checks of all saved partial graphs and necessary clauses. [Hall constraints](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_tip_hall.md) and [the tip PSD condition](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_tip_psd.md) constrain the nine matching-edge tips without assuming they form a rook graph.
- [Exact PSD checks of the Hall-run tips](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_tip_psd_hall.md) give five negative integer vectors and 26 positive rational congruences. The five rejected tips already violate common-neighbor caps. A signed-incidence factorization proves PSD automatically whenever the tip graph has no edge in common with the original rook graph.
- [A near-edge tip counterexample](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_tip_near_edge.md) passes all Hall masks, all tip common-neighbor caps, and exact PSD. These necessary conditions cannot force the tip and rook edge sets to be disjoint.
- [The complete single-pair edge model](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_iso_pair_full_caps.md) encodes all exterior common-neighbor caps. Its audited 1,556,032-clause formula has a bounded UNKNOWN outcome.
- [The eighteen-edge tip boundary](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_tip_rook_boundary.md) exhausts all 5,040 labelled rook tip graphs and reduces the incidence problem to 19 labelled cases in two core orbits. Both complete domains are exported for unrestricted incidence selection.
- [Further single-pair structure](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_pair_structural.md) gives an exact signed compression of rank at most 28 and a complete sixteen-design incidence subcase. Integer Farkas certificates exclude fourteen designs; the other two share a rational mixed-edge relaxation witness.
- [The checked boundary integer proof](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_tip_boundary_joint_integer.md) excludes the eighteen-member core orbit with all incidence choices free. Only the complementary-rook tip graph remains at eighteen edges; its joint run is UNKNOWN. [The edge-gap corollary](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_tip_edge_bound.md) gives `e(Z)<=16` or `Z=J-I-R`, and `4e(Z)+t<=72`.
- [Exclusion of the remaining a=0 incidence](research/grids-and-triangles/two_rook_a0_fixed_completion.md) combines the earlier fourteen Farkas exclusions with a complete binary-edge DRAT proof for the last two equivalent designs. The separate [a=1 incidence proof](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_a_incidence.md) strengthens the necessary bound to `a>=2`.
- [The complete a=2 incidence census](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_a2_incidence_census.md) has 576 designs in four orbits of size 144. Exact orbit regeneration and a final blocked-formula DRAT proof establish completeness. [Four checked complete-edge exclusions](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_a2_fixed_completion.md) rule out every design, strengthening the complementary-rook bound to `a>=3`.
- [The full-domain real projection](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_joint_projection.md) has exact primal and dual witnesses at `a=36`. Its incidence, mixed-edge, and core-pair-packing constraints do not improve the elementary upper bound on `a`; binary and further common-neighbor restrictions remain necessary.
- [Binary single-source projections](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_row_projection.md) retain the full incidence design and the perfect matching in one source neighborhood. All three signature orbits have independently replayed partial witnesses. This projection excludes no signature type.
- [The forced duplicate-pair projection](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_duplicate_row_projection.md) covers all 21 possible tip pairs in eight exact relabeling orbits. Both source neighborhoods are complete and disjoint. Every orbit has a directly replayed partial witness, so no tip pair is excluded by these conditions.
- [Eight full joint two-tip splits](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_joint_split.md) retain every incidence selector and eligible exterior edge. All eight thirty-second runs returned UNKNOWN. Their mixed and packing constraints omit general exterior pair caps.
- [Exact complementary-rook projector tests](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_projector_schur.md) forbid 1,296 exterior triangles in nine orbits. Every clause has a direct integer quadratic witness and a checked relabeling expansion. No signature or edge loses all triangle support.
- [Shared exterior-triangle constraints](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_triangle_constraints.md) count the full triangle domain and describe a stronger binary encoding. [Incidence-master controls](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_incidence_master_plan.md) verify signed-core proof semantics on a complete small instance; their scope is separate from actual graph exclusions.
- [Eight checked incidence-pattern exclusions](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_incidence_master_pilot_01_results.md) have complete-base rebuild and DRAT replays. [Relabeling closure](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_incidence_master_cut_orbits.md) yields 1,152 distinct signed clauses. The incidence class is not exhausted.
- [Exact incidence implications](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_incidence_core_simplification.md) shorten all eight patterns using quota traces and 34 small proofs. A separate [eleven-positive-signature exclusion](research/grids-and-triangles/complement_rook_positive_pattern.md) has a fresh full-model proof and independent replay. Its 144 relabelings require no negative assumptions.
- [Grid bounds, percolation, and triangle pairs](research/grids-and-triangles/grid_intersections.md) proves the baseline bounds of 120 grids in total and 15 through any vertex, at least 1,998 percolating nonedges, and at least 1,188 disjoint triangle pairs joined by exactly two edges. The finite enumerations and the 21-grid exclusion are included in `make verify`. [Run logs](experiments/logs/grids-and-triangles/)
- [Three rook-isomorphic bases are impossible](research/grids-and-triangles/three_red_boundary.md) excludes three disjoint grids with pairwise isomorphic matching interfaces. Its three cases use exact boundary counts and an integer incidence contradiction. It independently gives at most one all-red grid. [Translation certificate](research/grids-and-triangles/three_red_translation_incidence.md)
- [An exact aggregate certificate](research/grids-and-triangles/grid_count_119.md) proves `29g-4b<=3465`, hence at most 119 grids and at least 2,016 percolating nonedges using the preceding all-red bound. The parallel [root-grid branch](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/conway-99/pull/3) has the stronger total bound of 112 after its full-triangle component census.
- [Nine-vertex grid partitions](research/grids-and-triangles/affine_grids.md) studies a construction using eleven disjoint grids and an obstruction to a particular affine matching assignment.
- [A grid with its center missing](research/grids-and-triangles/incomplete_grid.md) derives an exterior-incidence reduction for an eight-vertex core.
- [Earlier bound of three all-red grids](research/grids-and-triangles/red_grid_three_bound.md) exhausts four-grid matching interfaces, rejects 124 assignments by integer Gram vectors, and excludes the remaining core by its outside-neighborhood count.
- [Earlier bound of seven all-red grids](research/grids-and-triangles/eight_red_exclusion.md) uses exact cycle spectra and covering certificates. [Nine-grid preliminary argument](research/grids-and-triangles/red_grid_limit.md)
- [Cubic trace closure](research/grids-and-triangles/red_grid_cubic_trace.md) gives a second, shorter proof of the seven-grid bound, using the cycle census and no three-cover enumeration.
- [The seven-grid remainder](research/grids-and-triangles/seven_red_residual_reduction.md) gives a spectral restriction on its triangle counts. The [four-cover exclusion](research/grids-and-triangles/seven_red_four_cover_reduction.md) and a [negative rook-space inequality](research/grids-and-triangles/seven_red_four_rook_exclusion.md) leave only 18 or 27 vertices in exactly two internal triangles.
- [No induced ternary Hamming cube](research/grids-and-triangles/eight_red_hamming.md) excludes an induced `H(3,3)` by boundary and common-neighbor counting.
- [Earlier aggregate grid-line constraints](research/grids-and-triangles/grid_flag_relaxation.md) records an exact integral count witness at 120 grids with the old bound of seven all-red grids. It does not satisfy the later all-red bound.
- [Incomplete-grid completion constraints](research/unrestricted-completion.md) proves a conditional sixteen-vertex independent set and records exact signature and partial-assignment exclusions. All eight starting cores remain unresolved.
- [Complete triangle stars](research/grids-and-triangles/triangle_star.md) derives a 48-vertex shell from an independent exterior signature.
- [Exact identities for an unrestricted triangle neighborhood](research/grids-and-triangles/triangle_root_constraints.md) describe all core Gram kernels, the false-twin cap criterion, automatic principal projector tests, and the exact exterior triangle count. The note distinguishes these identities from unproved completion feasibility.
