# A construction using eleven nine-vertex grids

Assume there is a partition into eleven induced 3-by-3 rook graphs. Each outside vertex sees exactly one vertex of a given grid. No two grid vertices can share another common neighbor, and the 9·10 edges leaving the grid have 90 exterior endpoints. Between every two grids there is a perfect matching. On the subspace constant on each grid the adjacency quotient is 3I+J. This partition is a construction assumption.

Give each grid coordinates in the affine plane of order three, using two of the four directions for its internal rook adjacency. A further construction assumption is that the intergrid matchings are affine bijections. Write their linear parts as a connection on the edges of the complete graph on eleven grid labels. Parallel transport along a closed walk gives an element of GL(2,3).

## Excluding an invariant dual direction

Suppose the linear holonomy preserves a direction in the dual plane. Transport it to one dual line in each grid and choose a nonzero linear functional there. The two complex characters given by that functional and its negative in each grid form an invariant space of dimension 22. The diagonal part of the adjacency on its two characters in cell i is d_i = 1 or −2, because a nonzero functional vanishes on either one or neither of the two chosen rook directions. An intercell matching either preserves or interchanges the two signs, with phases among the cube roots of unity. The constant matrix J vanishes on this space, so its adjacency operator X obeys X²+X=12I. If t cells have d_i=1, the trace forces the multiplicity of eigenvalue 3 to be (6t+44)/7. Thus t is 2 or 9.

Represent the character-line reversals on edges by minus signs. Gauge changes at vertices switch signs. For edge ij let u_ij count triangles through it with negative sign product. In the matrix entry connecting the opposite sign to the direct matching, the sum of u_ij cube roots must vanish. Hence u_ij is divisible by three. Switch the signs at vertices so all edges at label 0 are positive. If B is the adjacency matrix of the negative graph on the other ten labels, the edges 0i give deg_B(i)=0 modulo three. For ij among these ten, counting the modulo-three number of negative products yields (B²+B)_ij=0 modulo three. Indeed, for a positive edge the count is deg(i)+deg(j)−2(B²)_ij; for a negative edge it is 9−(deg(i)+deg(j)−2−2(B²)_ij). The diagonal follows from the degree condition.

[`grid_signs.py`](../../experiments/workspace/grid_signs.py) enumerates all sorted degree sequences in {0,3,6,9} and constructs the rows. Future vertices with identical completed incidence and final degree are interchangeable. This gives ten normalized graph classes and six switching classes with negative-triangle counts 0,46,73,92,119,165.

Normalize the phase of each remaining edge equation by the direct matching. The 9−u_ij matching-sign phases have sum b=−1−d_i−d_j. The real unit occurs (9−u_ij+2b)/3 times, the two other roots (9−u_ij−b)/3 times each. Exhausting both possible t and their 55 diagonal placements in [`grid_coordinate.py`](../../experiments/workspace/grid_coordinate.py) excludes every nontrivial switching class by nonnegativity alone. In the balanced class, the count of real units at an edge is 1 for two positive diagonal cells, 3 for mixed cells, and 5 for two negative cells. The real unit corresponds to a zero triangle voltage and occurs on all three edges of the triangle. The total must be divisible by three. It equals 235 if t=2 and 95 if t=9, both impossible. Consequently an affine matching construction would have to have irreducible linear holonomy. No automorphism of the 99 vertices was assumed in this exclusion.
