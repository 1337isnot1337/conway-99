## Root and high classes

Label the star leaves \(0,1,2,3\), its isolated vertex \(4\), and its center \(5\). Thus root type 42 is \(K_{1,4}\) plus an isolated vertex, with adjacency rows
\[

There are ten allowed high masks:
\[
A_{ij}=\{i,j,4\}\quad(0\le i<j\le3),
\qquad
B_i=\{i,4,5\}\quad(0\le i\le3).
\]
Each class is Boolean because two vertices in one class would have at least three common neighbors.

Interpret the selected \(A_{ij}\) classes as the edges of a simple graph \(A\) on the four leaves, and let \(b_i\) indicate selection of \(B_i\). The complete visible presence conditions are
\[
d_A(i)+b_i\le2\quad(i=0,1,2,3),
\qquad
\sum_i b_i\le2.
\]
The first equations are the residual common-neighbor bounds for root pairs \(\{4,i\}\); the second is the bound for \(\{4,5\}\). All remaining visible conditions are automatic for these masks and the independent high-vertex extension. The scope source checks this equivalence on all 1,024 subsets of the ten masks.

Write \(e=|E(A)|\) and \(b=\sum b_i\). Then
\[
2e+b\le8,\qquad b\le2.
\]
For \(b=0\), at most four high classes are selected. For \(b=1\), \(e\le3\), so the total is again at most four. For \(b=2\), \(e\le3\), giving maximum population five.

The selected \(A\)-edges therefore form a Hamiltonian path on the four leaves; the two selected \(B\)-classes are its endpoints. There are exactly \(4!/2=12\) such states, forming one \(S_4\) orbit. ## The one-scalar reduction

Let \(\Omega\) be the complete 253-atom domain, and let \(m_T\) be the labelled-root presence moment for \(T\). All \(m_T\) with \(|T|\le4\) have exact order-ten induced-count forms.

The twelve five-element states form a single root orbit. Their presence moments are equal because root relabelling permutes the complete set of labelled root embeddings. This is a relabelling identity, with no assumed automorphism of the hypothetical 99-vertex graph. Denote the common moment by \(u\).

Since population five is maximal, the presence moment of a five-element state is its exact-state mass. For every \(A\in\Omega\), define
\[
c_A=\sum_{\substack{T\in\Omega,\ T\supseteq A\\|T|\le4}}
(-1)^{|T|-|A|}m_T,
\qquad
n_A=\#\{T\in\Omega:|T|=5,\ T\supseteq A\}.
\]
Möbius inversion gives every exact-state mass:
\[
w_A=c_A+(-1)^{5-|A|}n_Au.
\]
For a five-element state, this is \(w_A=u\).

The preparation stores every sparse induced-count form \(c_A\), all slopes, and the explicit lists of fifth-state supersets. It verifies all 253 moment equations and all 23 quotient equations coefficientwise. It also checks that the affine forms are constant on root orbits.

## Stabilizer divisibility

For an exact high-set \(A\), let \(G_A\le\operatorname{Aut}(H)\) be its stabilizer. This group acts by precomposition on the injective labelled root embeddings realizing exactly \(A\). It preserves that set of embeddings. The action is free: if an injective map \(f\) satisfies \(f\circ g=f\), then \(g\) fixes every root vertex.

Therefore
\[
|G_A|\mid w_A.
\]
For the five-element states, \(|G_A|=2\), so \(u\) must be even.

This argument alone does not show that every formal coefficient of \(c_A\) is divisible by \(|G_A|\). The preparation checks that additional property exactly: every coefficient in every saved induced-count form \(c_A\) has the required divisor. It also verifies
\[
|G_A|\mid 2(-1)^{5-|A|}n_A.
\]
Consequently an even integer \(u\) supplies all 253 stabilizer divisibilities. No additional congruence is hidden in the prepared presence model.

The finite group checks include all stabilizers, their orbit-stabilizer identities, and 6,072 free-action controls on the 24 literal ordered root embeddings. The mathematical free-action proof applies to embeddings in any host graph.

## Exact query criterion

Set \(d_A=(-1)^{5-|A|}n_A\). The nonnegativity requirements are
\[
c_A+d_Au\ge0.
\]
For \(d_A=0\), require \(c_A\ge0\). The other requirements give the rational interval
\[
L=\max_{d_A>0}\frac{-c_A}{d_A},
\qquad
U=\min_{d_A<0}\frac{c_A}{-d_A}.
\]
The five-element atoms include the bound \(u\ge0\). The constant moment gives a finite upper bound.

A nonnegative real distribution exists exactly when every zero-slope condition holds and \(L\le U\). A distribution satisfying all integer and stabilizer conditions exists exactly when
\[
2\left\lceil\frac L2\right\rceil\le U
\]
and the zero-slope conditions hold. The source compares all fractions by exact BigInt cross multiplication and retains the active lower and upper atoms.

If the real interval is empty, multiplying its two active atom inequalities by positive integer slopes eliminates \(u\) and gives a direct exact order-ten count inequality. A zero-slope failure also gives a direct count inequality. If only the even-integer condition fails, the source records the congruence obstruction separately.

A successful query would provide all 253 integer atom weights, all 241 supplied lower moments, the twelve equal fifth moments, every stabilizer congruence, and all 23 quotient equations. It would remain a finite local presence-model certificate.
