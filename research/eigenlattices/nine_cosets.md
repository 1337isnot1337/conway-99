# Nine translated lattices and the maximal even extension

Use the notation of [`level_seven.md`](level_seven.md). Put K₀=√7 P A₉₈, p=√7P e₁, H₀=E∩Z⁹⁹ and w=√7p. For j modulo nine write Kⱼ=K₀+jp and Hⱼ=H₀+jw. Vectors in Hⱼ have coordinates in Z+j/9 and exhaust those vectors in E. For x∈E having constant coordinate residue t, applying A+4I shows 18t integral and summing coordinates shows 99t integral, hence 9t integral. A point y∈Kⱼ also has √7y in Hⱼ. The forms H and K of the earlier note are unions of classes 0,3,6. Indices d=9‖x‖²/2 in the series of Hⱼ and Kⱼ have residues 8j² and 5j² modulo nine, respectively.

The integral condition on graph realizations also admits a useful relaxation in which the two seven-adic elementary discriminants are fixed. Starting with K, choose a maximal totally isotropic subspace of its discriminant form and adjoin the corresponding vectors in K*. The resulting even lattice V has determinant 49: its seven-primary discriminant space is anisotropic, hence has dimension at most two over the finite field. Its dimension has even parity and cannot be zero because a positive definite even unimodular lattice has dimension divisible by eight. Then U=√7 V* lies inside H, and √7V lies inside U. The scalar bounds that use short-vector upper counts in H and the prescribed short points in K still apply to U and V. In particular, every norm-two vector in V gives a norm-14 signed Fano vector in H on multiplying by √7. Distinct such vectors up to sign are mutually orthogonal: any three common positive sites determine the Fano core, including both sides, so the dot product of two distinct oriented cores up to sign has absolute value at most four. For two root lifts in √7V the dot product is divisible by seven.

The nine residue classes persist. Define V₀ by requiring √7y integral in V, and put Vⱼ=V₀+jp. The map recording the residue of √7y in V has order three because V contains √7P of a triangle indicator. Thus V₀ has index three in V. Put U₀=U∩Z⁹⁹ and Uⱼ=U₀+jw. Indeed U contains 3w since 3p∈K⊂V. Pairings of y∈V₀ with w/√7=p are integers. As a result √7 V₀* is the union of the nine Uⱼ: it contains U and w, which generates a subgroup of order three modulo U, and the index over U is three. These classes remain in the original coordinate residue classes. For y∈Uⱼ, the condition that its squared norm divided by two is zero modulo seven forces y∈√7 Vⱼ. Subtract jw first, or localize at seven where the factor three is a unit, to apply anisotropy of the discriminant of V. Conversely membership in √7 Vⱼ implies the stated congruence. Thus the coefficient at index 7d in Uⱼ equals the one at d in Vⱼ. This gives a single maximal-lattice relaxation necessary regardless of the actual graph's modular rank. The preceding 34–42 rank restriction still concerns the actual graph.

A vector x=a+1/9 in H₁ satisfies sum a=−11 and (A+4I)a=−2·1. Write k=sum aᵢ(aᵢ+1)/2. If k≤4, an entry at most −2 would need positive neighbor mass at least six, which is unavailable. With minimum −1 and only positive unit entries there are t≤4 of them and 11+t negative sites. Each negative site needs at least two positive neighbors, but the pairs of positive sites have at most 2 binom(t,2) common-neighbor incidences. With a positive two, there is at most one other positive site. All negative sites then see the two, which at that site would require at least thirteen negative neighbors; the eigenvector equation requires at most eleven. Thus k≥5 and the squared norm is at least 178/9. For H₂,H₃,H₄, the coordinate-sum bounds alone give 154/9, 22, 220/9. The indicated bounds imply respective minima of at least 28/9,22/9,4,34/9 in V₁,V₂,V₃,V₄ after applying √7 and the required residue of the squared norm.

## Modular and coordinate tests

For an even unimodular auxiliary space of dimension a positive multiple of eight, take a vector v of norm eight nonzero modulo three and let T be the index-three sublattice with dot products with v divisible by three. Its discriminant group is cyclic of order nine generated by v/3. Glue the nine translates Tⱼ=T+jv/3 to Vⱼ. Their norms give an even integral lattice of determinant 49. Its √7-scaled dual is the union of the translates Uⱼ orthogonally summed with √7 Tⱼ: the fractional pairing contributions on the two factors are j/9 and 8j/9. At general initial rank r the same construction gives determinants 7^(44−r) and 7^(r+dim T). The script [`nine_cosets.py`](../../experiments/workspace/nine_cosets.py) uses scalar theta series, homogeneous harmonic polynomials in the squared lengths of the two factors, and point-coordinate zonal harmonics. [`coset_check.py`](../../experiments/workspace/coset_check.py) tests the gluing transform in 25 auxiliary examples. [`harmonic_check.py`](../../experiments/workspace/harmonic_check.py) checks separate harmonic transforms through degree fourteen.

For each vertex coordinate use t=9xᵢ on U, or t=9√7yᵢ on V. Translation by the site's distinguished vector changes t by 28 and the class by one. The change in d is 7(t+14) on U or t+14 on V. The signed histogram has total mass 99 times the shell count, mean zero, and second moment 18d times the count on U, seven times as large on V. For ordered vertex pairs the two translation vectors act on the marks by the matrix with diagonal 28 and off-diagonal −8 for an edge, 1 otherwise. The marginal neighbor sum conditional on t equals −4t for adjacent partners and 3t for nonadjacent partners, multiplied by the number of occurrences of t. [`coset_pair_flags.py`](../../experiments/workspace/coset_pair_flags.py) implements these constraints.

The single-coordinate maximal relaxation at order 20, including harmonic point weights of degrees through ten, has an integral solution to the tested coefficient inequalities. [`theta_integrality.py`](../../experiments/workspace/theta_integrality.py) imposed all available integral shell counts, centrally symmetric shell parities, and symmetry at marks 0 and 14. The rational inequalities and the integrality lattice were replayed on its integer witness. This solution supplies aggregate shell counts only.
