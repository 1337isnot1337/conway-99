# The companion lattice in the 3-eigenspace

Let F be the 54-dimensional 3-eigenspace, Q the orthogonal projection onto F, and M=F∩Z⁹⁹. We have 7Q=4I+A−2J/11. At seven the constant line splits off unimodularly, so the primitive lattices in the two other eigenspaces have opposite discriminant forms there. At eleven the sum of the constant line and F splits off unimodularly; taking the orthogonal complement of the primitive constant vector of norm 99 gives the cyclic eleven-part. The projector has no other denominators. Thus det M=11·7ʳ, with the same r as in [`level_seven.md`](level_seven.md). The lattice M is even since its points are integral and have coordinate sum zero.

Put B₀=√7 Q A₉₈, p=√7 Qe₁ and Bⱼ=B₀+jp for residues modulo eleven. The projection kernel admits only coordinate sums divisible by eleven, by 7Q's fractional part; (A−3I)eᵢ provides sum eleven in the kernel. Put Mⱼ=F∩(Z⁹⁹+(j/11)1). If x belongs to √7 B₀*, its coordinates have the same residue because it pairs integrally with all coordinate differences. The 3-eigenvalue equation gives 11 times this residue integral. All eleven translates occur since 7Qe₁ has residue −2/11. B₀ has discriminant 11·7^(54−r) by duality. Vectors of Bⱼ have fractional squared norm divided by two congruent to −j²/11, while the corresponding fraction in Mⱼ is j²/11.

A nonzero integral 3-eigenvector must have at least six positive sites and at least six negative sites. On either sign support the positive coordinate magnitudes form a subeigenvector for the principal adjacency matrix with eigenvalue three. Every graph on at most five sites has spectral radius strictly less than three, as in [`level_seven.md`](level_seven.md). At norm twelve the two sides are anticomplete triangular prisms. Among six-site permissible graphs only the triangular prism has largest eigenvalue at least three; [`six_coset_floors.py`](../../experiments/workspace/six_coset_floors.py) checks the other 61 cases by exact leading minors. All prisms with the same projection into the negative eigenspace are disjoint, since a common point would leave a 3-eigenvector with smaller sign support. For two prisms in such a class the numbers of neighbors in them agree at exterior points, so they have identical sets of three triangle tips on their vertical matching edges. Each tip has fourteen neighbors, two from each prism, so a class has size at most seven. There are at most 1386 prisms (for each triangle at most twelve triangles matching it, and divide by two). Hence the norm-12 shell has at most six times the prism count.

At norm fourteen both signs occupy seven unit sites. Each side has induced minimum degree at least three. Exactly one seven-vertex type has this property: it has a single degree-four center, four neighbors forming two matched pairs around that center, and two remaining vertices occupying opposite cells of those four near labels. The two degree-four centers must be adjacent. An ordered pair of possible centers has 1386 choices, and at most 42 choices per center specify its side by choosing two near axes and one of two opposite pairs. This gives the shell upper bound 1386·42².

All sparse lifts of a fixed sum on at most six sites project to different points of Bⱼ. Otherwise their difference is a nonzero integral −4 eigenvector supported on at most twelve sites. More generally no real −4 eigenvector has such small support. Let its signs have sizes m≤n and let C be the m-by-n cross adjacency. For the positive magnitude vector z on the smaller side, 16z≤CCᵀz. The row sums of CCᵀ are at most n+2(m−1)≤10+m≤16. Equality is possible only when m=n=6; propagation on the positive Perron component would require every row on it to see six opposite neighbors and all five other rows with codegree two. All six rows would then be full, contradicting the bound on pair codegrees. This gives the stated injection even for lifts with repeated coordinates.

The binary lattice with Gram matrix [[2,1],[1,6]] and dual generator the second dual basis vector has discriminant eleven and generator norm 2/11. Denote its cosets by Tⱼ. Joining Bⱼ to Tⱼ gives an even rank-56 lattice with determinant 7^(54−r). Its scaled dual joins Mⱼ to √7 T_(5j), because the two generator pairings have residues j/11 and twice the auxiliary index divided by eleven. [`positive_theta.py`](../../experiments/workspace/positive_theta.py) implements scalar and harmonic identities for these joins and lower bounds from all sparse lifts on six sites. This work supplies necessary conditions.
